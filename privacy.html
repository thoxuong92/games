Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

What we collect

The games have no analytics. Fish Hunter, Cờ Tướng - Chinese Chess, Minesweeper, Caro, Chess 3D and Tetra Pick talk to our game server (run on Cloudflare) to let you play with others, to keep your account and progress or to show leaderboards; it receives only what is listed below.

Your account

In Fish Hunter, Cờ Tướng - Chinese Chess, Minesweeper and Tetra Pick, every player gets an account the first time the game connects: at first a guest account with a name made up by the game. One account works for all of these games, on the web and inside Discord. You can register it with an email address or a username and a password, or sign in with Discord, to keep your progress and play on other devices. Our account server keeps, in a database hosted by Hostinger: your account id and display name; your username and email address if you gave them; your password only as a salted hash (never the password itself); your Discord user id and name if you linked Discord; your progress in each game (such as coins, items, levels, missions and settings); your ratings and leaderboard scores; and your sign-ins (a hashed session token, your browser's name and when it was last used). To stop password guessing it also counts sign-in attempts per IP address and per username or email for a short time (up to an hour). We do not send you emails and do not sell or share this data.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (such as coins, daily gifts, missions and settings like sound) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. In Fish Hunter, Cờ Tướng - Chinese Chess, Minesweeper, Caro, Chess 3D and Tetra Pick, a player name and a random player id are saved there too; outside Discord the name is made up by the game. A random session token keeps you signed in to your account; with it the progress is copied to your account a few seconds after it changes. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Playing at a table

When you enter a room in Fish Hunter, Cờ Tướng - Chinese Chess and Chess 3D, the game connects to our game server to seat you at a table with other players. It sends your player name and what you do in the game (such as your shots, what you catch and the looks you use). The other players at the table see your name and your play. Tables keep no record of this after they close.

When you enter a room in Caro, the game connects to our game server to seat you at a table with other players. It sends your player name and the moves you make; the other players at the table see your name and your moves.

Match history

Caro keeps the moves and results of finished matches on our game server, so that players can replay them. Other players can see this history with the names of the players in each match.

Leaderboards

Fish Hunter, Cờ Tướng - Chinese Chess, Minesweeper, Caro and Tetra Pick keep leaderboards on our game server (such as today's and this week's best scores, or players' ratings): your account id, your display name and your score or rating. Other players see the names and scores at the top of each board.

Discord

When you play Fish Hunter, Cờ Tướng - Chinese Chess and Caro inside Discord as an Activity, the game asks Discord once for permission to read your basic account details (Discord's "identify" permission). With it the game uses your Discord user id and display name: the name is shown to other players and on the leaderboards, and the id signs you in to your account (the same account on every device). We do not request or receive your email, messages or friends list. The other games use only the Discord features that need no sign-in permission. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.

Ads and game portals

When a game is played on a third-party portal (for example Poki or CrazyGames) or another platform, that portal may show ads and process data under its own privacy policy. The games show ads only through the portal's own system.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the game server runs on Cloudflare; the account database is hosted by Hostinger. They may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under their own privacy statements.

Children

The games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your data and contact

You can delete your account with all its progress, ratings and scores yourself at game.joukyuu.com/account/. To have your name and score removed from the leaderboards, or for any question about your data, email nguyenhaktpm2k5@gmail.com with the player name you play under (your Discord name when you play in Discord). Data stored on your device you can delete yourself, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
