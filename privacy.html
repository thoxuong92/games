Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

What we collect

The games have no accounts, no email sign-up and no analytics. Fish Hunter talks to our game server (run on Cloudflare) to let you play with others or to show leaderboards; it receives only what is listed below. The other games send nothing to any server of ours.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (such as coins, daily gifts, missions and settings like sound) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. In Fish Hunter, a player name and a random player id are saved there too; outside Discord the name is made up by the game. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Playing at a table

When you enter a room in Fish Hunter, the game connects to our game server to seat you at a table with other players. It sends your player name and what you do in the game (such as your shots, what you catch and the looks you use). The other players at the table see your name and your play. Tables keep no record of this after they close.

Leaderboards

Fish Hunter reports your score to our game server, which keeps today's and this week's leaderboards: your player id, your player name and your score. Other players see the names and scores at the top of each board.

Discord

When you play Fish Hunter inside Discord as an Activity, the game asks Discord once for permission to read your basic account details (Discord's "identify" permission). With it the game uses your Discord user id and display name: the name is shown to other players and on the leaderboards, and the id keeps your leaderboard place the same on every device. We do not request or receive your email, messages or friends list. The other games use only the Discord features that need no sign-in permission. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.

Ads and game portals

When a game is played on a third-party portal (for example Poki or CrazyGames) or another platform, that portal may show ads and process data under its own privacy policy. The games show ads only through the portal's own system.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the game server runs on Cloudflare. They may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under their own privacy statements.

Children

The games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your data and contact

To have your name and score removed from the leaderboards, or for any question about your data, email nguyenhaktpm2k5@gmail.com with the player name you play under (your Discord name when you play in Discord). Data stored on your device you can delete yourself, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
