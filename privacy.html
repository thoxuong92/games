Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The games on game.joukyuu.com have no accounts and do not send your data to any server of ours.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, daily gift streak, missions, settings such as sound) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It stays there, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Discord

When you play inside Discord as an Activity, the game runs in Discord and uses only the Discord features that need no sign-in permission (such as sharing a link). We do not request or receive your Discord account details, messages or friends list. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.

Ads and game portals

When a game is played on a third-party portal (for example Poki or CrazyGames) or another platform, that portal may show ads and process data under its own privacy policy. The games show ads only through the portal's own system: when you choose to watch one for a reward, or when you enter or leave a room.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
