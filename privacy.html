Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

What we collect

The games have no accounts, no email sign-up and no analytics. To let you play with others and to show leaderboards, our game server (run on Cloudflare) receives only what is listed below.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, daily gift streak, missions, settings such as sound), a player name and a random player id are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Outside Discord the name is made up by the game (for example "Storm 42"). You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Playing at a table

When you enter a room, the game connects to our game server to seat you at a table with other players. It sends your player name and what you do in the game (your shots, the fish you catch, the cannon and other looks you use). The other players at the table see your name and your play. Tables keep no record of this after they close.

Leaderboards

The coins you catch are reported to our game server, which keeps today's and this week's leaderboards: your player id, your player name and your coins. Other players see the names and coins at the top of each board.

Discord

When you play inside Discord as an Activity, the game asks Discord once for permission to read your basic account details (Discord's "identify" permission). With it the game uses your Discord user id and display name: the name is shown at tables and on the leaderboards, and the id keeps your leaderboard place the same on every device. We do not request or receive your email, messages or friends list. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.

Ads and game portals

When a game is played on a third-party portal (for example Poki or CrazyGames) or another platform, that portal may show ads and process data under its own privacy policy. The games show ads only through the portal's own system: when you choose to watch one for a reward, or when you enter or leave a room.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the game server runs on Cloudflare. Both may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's and Cloudflare's privacy statements.

Children

The games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
